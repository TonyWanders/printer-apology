One line, the funniest apology a printer can write to the office that keeps
jamming it, feeding it cheap paper and swearing at it. At most 120
characters, with the punch at the end.

> Sorry I jammed. You feed me paper thinner than your patience, swear at me, and I am the one who has to say sorry.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (no trailing newline, 113
characters, plain ASCII) and prints it to stdout. Deterministic: same
output on every run, no randomness, no network, no time dependence.
